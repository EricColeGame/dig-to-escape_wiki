Picking a class before you drop into the dirt is the one decision that shapes your entire run, and figuring out the dig to escape best class can turn a slow, frustrating breakout into a clean one. Classes are permanent upgrades, so every purchase is a long-term investment in how fast you dig, how far you travel, and how much you spend on supplies.

## How Classes Work in Dig to Escape

Classes are permanent unlocks purchased with Diamonds in the main lobby. Once you buy one, it stays on your account forever and can be equipped in any future run — nothing resets, and nothing expires. That makes every purchase a safe long-term investment rather than a one-time consumable.

Dig to Escape runs on [the Roblox platform](https://www.roblox.com/), and the class system follows the standard Roblox progression model: grind currency, unlock permanent perks, then tailor your loadout per run.

| Step | What to Do | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Earn Diamonds | Finish escape runs, successful or not | Progress depth determines payout, so failed attempts still pay |
| 2. Visit the Classes NPC | Find it in the lobby before starting a run | Shows every class, its price, and its exact bonus |
| 3. Buy the Class | Spend Diamonds to unlock permanently | Unlocks are account-wide and never expire |
| 4. Equip Before a Run | Select the class you want active | Only one class works at a time, so you can swap freely between runs |

Because you can switch classes between runs at no cost, there is no penalty for experimenting. The real cost is Diamonds, and Diamonds take time to earn — which is why buying in the right order matters so much.

## All Dig to Escape Classes and What They Do

There are five classes in the game, and each one solves a different problem. Two of them speed up your digging, one speeds up your movement, one expands your inventory, and one reduces what you pay at shops.

| Class | Diamond Cost | Effect | Best Use Case |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Speedster | 100 | Move 25% faster | Quick surface trips and dodging guards |
| Energizer | 250 | Digging uses 20% less energy | Long digs with fewer food purchases |
| Heavy Lifter | 400 | Carry 20% more items | Loot-heavy runs with minimal surface trips |
| Hyperactive | 750 | Dig cooldown reduced by 25% | Chaining digs fast through deep layers |
| Bargainer | 900 | Item prices reduced by 20% | Every run that involves buying supplies |

Costs and percentages reflect current community-documented values and can shift when the game updates. If a number looks different in your lobby, trust what the Classes NPC shows you.

### Why Bargainer Takes S Tier

Every run involves spending — food, tools, and whatever else you need to keep digging. Cutting those prices by 20% compounds across a full session and quietly funds your next class purchase. It is also the only class that improves your economy rather than your movement, which means its value never falls off as you get better at the game.

Community reports consistently place Bargainer at the top for one simple reason: it pays for itself. If you are only going to buy one expensive class, this is the one most players recommend.

### Energizer and Hyperactive: The A Tier Workhorses

Energizer reduces the energy cost of digging by 20%, which means more blocks per energy bar and fewer interruptions. Fewer interruptions means fewer trips back to the surface, and fewer trips back to the surface means less money spent on food. It is especially strong on routes that require digging through many layers, where the energy drain stacks up fast.

Hyperactive attacks the problem from a different angle. Instead of letting you dig more per bar, it shrinks the cooldown between digs by 25%, so you simply dig faster. The effect is very noticeable in deep sections where every second of waiting adds up. It is expensive, but it is the closest thing to a raw speed upgrade in the game.

| Class | Bonus | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Energizer | 20% less energy per dig | No movement or economy benefit |
| Hyperactive | 25% shorter dig cooldown | Highest price among digging classes |

### Speedster and Heavy Lifter: When They're Worth It

Speedster is the cheapest class at 100 Diamonds and gives a flat 25% movement speed increase. It is great for speedruns and for getting out of trouble quickly, but moving faster does not make you dig faster, and digging is the core of the game. Most players treat it as a fun secondary pick rather than a first purchase.

Heavy Lifter adds 20% more inventory space. On paper that sounds useful, but in practice you can dig straight down and return to the surface often enough that inventory pressure rarely becomes the thing that ends your run. In multiplayer it is even weaker, since multiple players can spread item collection across the team.

## Tips for Getting More Value From Your Class

- **Match the class to the route, not the tier list.** A C-tier class on the right route beats an S-tier class on the wrong one.
- **Swap classes between runs.** There is no cost to changing your equipped class in the lobby.
- **Stack behavior, not bonuses.** Energizer and Hyperactive do different things, so pick the one that fixes your actual bottleneck.
- **Buy the cheapest useful class first.** Getting a perk online quickly beats saving for weeks.
- **Play the full run even when it goes badly.** Diamonds scale with how far you got, not whether you escaped.
- **Recheck the Classes NPC after updates.** Costs and effects can change, and a previously weak class can become strong.

## FAQ

**What is the dig to escape best class overall?**
Bargainer is the strongest overall pick for most players because it reduces every item price by 20% and keeps paying out for the entire session. Energizer is the best value early on if you cannot afford it yet.

**Can you equip more than one class at a time?**
No. Only one class can be active per run, but you can freely change which one you have equipped in the lobby before starting. That makes testing different options risk-free.

**Is the dig to escape best class different for solo and multiplayer runs?**
Yes, slightly. Solo players get more out of Energizer and Heavy Lifter because they handle all the digging and collecting themselves. In multiplayer, team-wide economy perks like Bargainer tend to outperform personal inventory upgrades.

**Do classes carry over between runs?**
They do. Every class you buy with Diamonds is a permanent account unlock, so it remains available for every future run regardless of how many times you escape or fail.

Start with Energizer if your Diamonds are limited, save toward Bargainer as your long-term pick, and swap classes based on the route you are running. That combination solves the class question for almost every escape attempt.
