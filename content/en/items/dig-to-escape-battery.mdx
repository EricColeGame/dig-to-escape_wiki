## What the Dig to Escape Battery Route Actually Requires

The Janitor's Ending is the second escape route in Dig to Escape, and it plays nothing like the classic dig-until-you-break-out path. Instead of grinding through dirt, you buy a Keycard, slip into the janitor's room, and crawl through a vent into a small back room. That room holds a switchboard, and the switchboard needs power. Power needs 12 batteries.

The catch is the hallway outside. A guard patrols it with a flashlight, and his pathing is genuinely unorthodox — one wrong step and your run is over. Everything before the batteries is just setup; everything after is a race to the elevator.

| Requirement | What It Does | How You Get It |

| 12 Batteries | Restores power to the elevator | Random spawns in the hall and side rooms |

## Dig to Escape Battery Locations: Where All 12 Spawn

Batteries are not fixed in Dig to Escape. They spawn randomly throughout the main hall and every room connected to it, which means no two runs look identical. That said, player experience points to a handful of spots that come up far more often than others. Sweep those first and you'll usually bank most of your twelve before the guard finishes a single patrol loop.

| Spawn Spot | Exact Location | How Often Reported | Risk Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Second room | Middle table beside the monitor | Very common | Medium |
| Circular table | End of the hallway | Very common | High |
| Cabin path | Along the cabin route | Common | Medium |
| Keycard room | Inside the locked side room | Common | Medium-High |
| Open hallway | Anywhere along the patrol route | Random | High |

One of those rooms is locked, and you need your Keycard equipped before you even reach the door. Standing there fumbling through your inventory while the guard closes in is a death sentence. Equip it in advance, scan the door immediately, and know what you're grabbing before you step inside. Precision beats speed here — the guard is fast, and hesitation is what gets players caught.

## Prep Work: Bank Toilet Paper Before You Touch a Battery

The battery run is short, but it's expensive to set up, and the money comes from digging. Use a rectangular clearing pattern instead of tunneling straight down — it exposes far more ground and surfaces far more sellable items. Consistent digging, eating, and selling nets roughly 20–30 Toilet Paper per day, which puts a 200 TP Keycard within about 10 in-game days.

| Task | Why It Matters | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Dig in rectangular blocks | Surfaces more items per swing | Ongoing |
| Sell everything you don't eat | Funds the Keycard | Daily |
| Trade only for food and energy | Stamina keeps digging fast | Daily |
| Skip Energy Surge, extra Storage, new Shovels | Unnecessary for this route | Saves TP |
| Scout the vents during "Afternoon" | Guards are less aggressive | One session |
| Avoid trading at night | Guard patrols aggressively | — |

The vent network is your best friend here. The vent inside your own cell drops you into the prison restroom, which connects to both the Janitor's Room and the Security Room next door. A separate vent in the main hallway gives you access to four nearby cells, which is handy for repositioning without crossing open ground. Do all of it during daylight — "Afternoon" is the safest window.

## Dig to Escape Battery Tips: Guard Timing and the Wall Trick

Speed matters less than positioning. The guard's route is strange enough that predicting him is unreliable, so build escape routes instead of trying to time perfect dashes. Keep a door or a corner between you and him whenever possible, and never commit to a long hallway run without an exit.

| Situation | Smart Move | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Guard approaching your room | Freeze and let him pass | Running for a new room |
| You need the Keycard room | Equip the Keycard before the door | Digging through your inventory |
| Battery at the hall's end | Grab it last, on the way back | Grabbing it first |
| Guard camping the left hall | Wait; he eventually rotates | Sprinting past him |
| You need to skip a risky room | Insert batteries through the wall | Entering the room at all |

Community reports describe a shortcut where you can insert batteries through the wall by standing in the room next to the switchboard. It removes an entire trip through the riskiest part of the map. Treat it as a bug rather than a feature — it may get patched, so don't build your whole strategy around it.

## Common Mistakes That Ruin the Battery Run

The dig to escape battery hunt rewards patience far more than reflexes. Treat it like a scavenger sweep, not a shopping trip. Twelve batteries is a lot of ground to cover with a guard breathing down your neck, and community reports suggest most failed runs come down to impatience in the first 30 seconds inside the switchboard room.

| Mistake | What Happens | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Digging straight down | Fewer items, slower Toilet Paper | Clear in rectangular blocks |
| Escaping at night | Guard catches you instantly | Go in the morning |
| Buying upgrades first | Can't afford the Keycard | Save 200 TP before anything else |
| Entering the switchboard room blind | Caught mid-search | Pre-plan your route |
| Forgetting the Keycard for the locked room | Wasted trip across the hall | Equip it early |
| Losing count of batteries | Miss one and stall at the switchboard | Count to 12 as you place them |

One more habit worth building: keep your inventory light. Carrying items you don't need slows down the decision-making that this route demands, and the window between patrols is short. Sell anything you're not actively using before you start the escape.

## FAQ

**How many batteries do you need for the dig to escape battery puzzle?**
Twelve. All 12 must be placed inside the switchboard before the power returns and the elevator opens. There's no partial credit — the switchboard stays dead until the count is complete.

**Do dig to escape battery spawns change every run?**
Yes. They spawn randomly across the main hall and the connected rooms. Common spots include the middle table near the monitor in the second room, the circular table at the end of the hall, the cabin path, and the Keycard-locked room — but none of them are guaranteed.

**Is the wall trick for inserting batteries real?**
Community reports say yes — standing in the room adjacent to the switchboard lets you insert batteries through the wall, skipping the switchboard room entirely. It's likely an unintended bug, so expect it to be patched at some point.

**What happens after you restore power?**
Wait for the guard to move away from the left side of the hall, then sprint to the bright open elevator. Once you step inside, the "JANITOR ENDING" screen appears along with your total gameplay time.
