## When Your Golden Spoon Breaks: Durability and Repair

The golden spoon is tough, but it isn't immortal. Breakage is one of the most common complaints in the community, and the confusion around it comes from the fact that the fix isn't always obvious.

| Symptom | Likely Cause | What Players Report Works |
|---|---|---|
| Spoon stops digging entirely | Durability hit zero | Swap to a backup tool and look for a repair option |
| Digging speed drops sharply | Tool is worn, not broken | Finish the layer and repair before it fails |
| Tool disappears from inventory | Break without a repair | Re-obtain through the same method you got it |
| Can't repair at any station | Wrong station or missing requirement | Check the upgrade vendor or event menu |

A few practical habits that reduce breakage:

- **Don't use the golden spoon on the hardest available layer.** Match the tool to the terrain. If blocks are taking multiple hits, you're overworking it.
- **Repair early.** Waiting until the durability bar is nearly empty is how tools break at the worst possible moment.
- **Keep a backup.** A cheap starter spoon in reserve means a broken golden spoon doesn't end your run.
- **Bank currency before you need it.** If a repair costs resources, you want them on hand, not grinding for them mid-crisis.
