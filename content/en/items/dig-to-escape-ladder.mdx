## What the Dig to Escape Ladder Actually Does

Despite the name of the game, the ladder is not a digging tool. It is a carryable climbing prop. You pick it up, haul it across the map, and set it down next to a specific vent inside the Janitor Closet. Once it is in place, that vent becomes reachable, and the vent is your way out of the facility.

This is why the item matters so much. A ladder dropped in a random hallway is dead weight that slows you down and does nothing. A ladder placed against the correct vent is the difference between a finished run and a restart.

| Question | Short answer |
|---|---|
| What is the ladder? | A carryable climbing prop, not a digging tool |
| Where does it go? | Beside the vent inside the Janitor Closet |
| What does it unlock? | Access to the vent, and from there the escape ending |
| What happens if you lose it? | The ladder route is usually dead for that run |

The takeaway is simple: never pick up the dig to escape ladder until you already know your route to the Janitor Closet. Carrying it blind is how most players get caught.

## Where to Find the Ladder

Published walkthroughs and community reports point to a small number of reliable ladder spots rather than random spawns. The most commonly described one sits inside the room with the sleeping guard — the ladder is on the left side of that room, and the guard will not react as long as you move quietly and avoid waking him.

Knowing the surrounding rooms matters just as much as knowing the spawn, because the ladder route runs through several connected spaces.

| Location | What you will find | Your move |
|---|---|---|
| Guard room | Ladder on the left side, guard asleep | Grab the ladder without waking him |
| Janitor Closet | The vent you need to reach | Place the ladder beside the vent |
| Vent interior | A crawl route leading out | Exit the vent |
| Office area | A table and a desk holding batteries | Collect the batteries before you leave |

A quick note on layouts: spawn positions in games like this can shift between updates, so treat the table above as the standard route rather than a guaranteed coordinate. Scout the guard room first on every run and confirm the ladder is where you expect it before you commit.

## Step-by-Step: The Janitor Closet Ladder Escape Route

This is the sequence that most players use to turn the dig to escape ladder into a completed ending. The order matters — grabbing the ladder before you have scouted the path is the single most common reason runs fall apart.

| Step | Action | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scout the guard room and the Janitor Closet first | You need to know the route before you are slowed down |
| 2 | Enter the guard room quietly and check that the guard is asleep | A woken guard turns a simple pickup into a chase |
| 3 | Pick up the ladder on the left side of the room | This is the most reliably reported spawn |
| 4 | Carry the ladder to the Janitor Closet | Do not detour — every extra room is extra risk |
| 5 | Place the ladder next to the vent | Placement is what activates the route |
| 6 | Climb up and exit through the vent | This is your way out of the locked area |
| 7 | Collect the batteries from the table and desk | Walkthroughs report 12 batteries are needed here |
| 8 | Escape | Finish the route and trigger the ending |

The battery step is the one people forget. According to IGN's Dig to Escape endings walkthrough, the sequence is to place the ladder by the vent in the Janitor Closet, exit the vent, collect the batteries from the table and desk, and then escape. Skip the batteries and you will be standing at the finish line with nothing to show for it.

A practical tip: do the battery pickup on the way if your route allows it. Backtracking with a guard on alert is far riskier than grabbing supplies early.

## Ladder Route vs. Chase Route: Which Ending Should You Aim For?

Dig to Escape has more than one way out, and the dig to escape ladder is not required for all of them. Community guides describe a second style of finish where a chase sequence starts and you simply run forward, dodging obstacles until you reach a ladder you can climb out on.

Both are valid. They just reward different skills.

| Route | Core item | Main skill tested | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Janitor Closet route | The ladder, placed by the vent | Stealth, route planning, item management | Patient players who like a clean run |
| Chase sequence route | None — you run for the exit ladder | Sprinting, dodging, stamina management | Players with a strong stamina build |

If you are new to the game, the Janitor Closet route is usually the friendlier option because it fails slowly. You can recover from a wrong turn. A chase sequence fails fast, and one clipped obstacle can end the run.

## Tips for Carrying the Ladder Without Getting Caught

Carrying the dig to escape ladder changes how you move. You are slower, you are easier to spot, and you cannot fight back. Treat the carry like a stealth section, not a sprint.

- **Walk, do not sprint, in guard-heavy rooms.** Sprinting drains energy you will want later.
- **Plan the full path before you pick the ladder up.** Stand in the guard room, look at the door you need, and commit.
- **Use sound to track the guard.** If you can hear him, he is close enough to matter.
- **Never set the ladder down "just for a second"** in an open area. It is easy to lose track of it.
- **Save your energy for the final stretch.** The last part of the route is where stamina decides the run.

Class choice also matters more than most players expect. Community advice consistently points toward picking a class that reduces energy drain, because the ladder route involves a lot of walking under load and the finish often turns into a run.

| Class trait | Why it helps on a ladder run | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Reduced energy drain | Keeps you moving during long carries and the final dash | High |
| Extra stamina capacity | Gives you a buffer if a guard spots you late | High |
| Movement or speed bonuses | Slightly faster carries, but no help if you run dry | Medium |
| Anything purely combat-focused | Almost no value on a stealth ladder route | Low |

If you only remember one thing from this section, make it this: energy management is the real boss of the dig to escape ladder route.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Where do you place the dig to escape ladder?**
Beside the vent inside the Janitor Closet. That placement is what makes the vent climbable and opens the route out. A ladder placed anywhere else will not progress the run.

**Where does the ladder spawn?**
The most reliably reported spawn is inside the room with the sleeping guard, on the left side of the room. Move quietly so you do not wake him while picking it up.

**Can you finish Dig to Escape without the ladder?**
Yes — the chase sequence route does not require it. That route asks you to run forward through a chase, dodging obstacles until you reach the exit ladder and climb out. It is faster but far less forgiving.

**How many batteries do you need after placing the ladder?**
Walkthroughs report that you need 12 batteries, collected from a table and a desk after you exit the vent. Grab them before you head for the exit, because they are required for the ending.

**Why is the dig to escape ladder so important?**
Because it converts a dead-end vent into a usable exit. Without it, the Janitor Closet route simply does not open, which makes the ladder the single most consequential item in that escape path.

Get the pickup, the carry, and the placement right, and the rest of the run becomes a straight line to the exit.
