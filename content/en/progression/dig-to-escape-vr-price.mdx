## The Progression Loop: How Digging Becomes Escape Power

Here's where your money actually goes. Dig to Escape is structured around a repeatable loop that converts physical effort in VR into permanent character power.

### Step 1: Dig and Mine

You swing a shovel, break through dirt and rock, and uncover valuable minerals. Early tunnels are shallow and slow because your starting tool is basic. Every swing teaches you the layout of your prison and the value of what's buried under it.

### Step 2: Sell and Upgrade

Mined minerals get sold, and the proceeds fund better tools. The store description is direct about the payoff: better gear means faster digging, deeper tunnels, and more escape options. That's a clean three-stage progression curve, and it's the spine of the whole game.

### Step 3: Survive the Complications

Two systems complicate the loop. A sneaky thief — the mole — targets your loot and will make off with hard-earned minerals if you don't catch him. Meanwhile, other inmates create a social layer where you can brawl, intimidate, or outsmart your way to the top. Optional quests can accelerate your escape or make everything far more dangerous.

| Progression Stage | What You're Doing | Upgrade Focus | Main Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early | Learning dig angles, collecting first minerals | Basic digging speed and reach | Slow progress, wasted swings |
| Mid | Selling consistently, funding real tool upgrades | Faster digging, deeper tunnels | The mole stealing your stockpile |
| Late | Pushing deep tunnels, planning the exit | Escape-route tools and route knowledge | Dangerous optional inmate quests |

| Upgrade Category | What It Unlocks | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Digging speed | More minerals per session | Highest |
| Digging depth | Access to richer deposits | High |
| Escape options | New ways out of the prison | Situational |
| Cosmetic shovel skins | Visual variety only | Lowest |

The takeaway: spend your in-game coins on tools that change what you can reach, not on how your shovel looks.

## Practical Tips to Progress Faster Without Spending More

The Dig to Escape VR price is fixed, but your progression speed isn't. These habits come from the game's own systems and from player experience in the review section.

- **Dig in planned grids, not random holes.** Consistent tunnel patterns make it easier to remember where you've been and where rich deposits were.
- **Bank minerals before long sessions.** The mole targets loose loot. Selling often reduces what's at risk.
- **Prioritize tool upgrades over everything else.** Faster digging compounds — every upgrade makes the next mineral run shorter.
- **Treat inmate quests as risk trades.** They can open escape options, but the store description warns they can also make your situation much more dangerous.
- **Play to your comfort setting.** Dig to Escape supports standing, sitting, and roomscale play, so a sitting session is a legitimate way to grind minerals without exhausting yourself.
- **Check for sales before buying add-ons.** Store-wide promotions are common on the Meta Quest platform, and cosmetic bundles are frequent discount candidates.
