## What Is the Dig to Escape Janitor Ending?

The Dig to Escape janitor ending is an alternate escape route that takes you out through the janitor's room instead of the main dig shaft. Instead of tunneling your way to the surface, you farm currency underground, buy an illegal key item, and slip out through a vent. Community reports consistently describe it as the second escape route most players unlock, and one of the friendliest options for solo runs.

So why does it matter? Because the dig to escape janitor ending rewards preparation over raw digging speed. You do not need a perfect route or a lucky item spawn. You need 200 Toilet Paper, one Illegal Card, and 12 batteries. That makes it a great "first alternate ending" to chase if you keep failing the main escape.

This guide breaks the whole route into checkpoints, shows what each stage actually costs you, and covers the mistakes that waste runs.

## Janitor Ending Requirements at a Glance

Before you commit a run to this route, know exactly what you are signing up for. The table below summarizes everything the janitor route demands, based on published walkthroughs and player experience.

| Requirement | Amount / Detail | Where You Get It |
|---|---|---|
| Toilet Paper | 200 | Selling dug-up items |
| Illegal Card | 1 purchase | Bought with Toilet Paper |
| Batteries | 12 | Collected inside the janitor area |
| Access point | Vent in the Janitor Room | Reached with the Illegal Card |
| Recommended class | Anything with fast digging or carry capacity | Class selection screen |
| Difficulty | Low to medium | Solo-friendly |

Two things stand out here. First, the currency grind is the only real bottleneck — everything after the Illegal Card is a short fetch sequence. Second, the battery count is specific: 12, not "a handful." If you walk into the vent area missing even one, you are walking back out.

### What the Illegal Card Actually Does

The Illegal Card is the gate. Without it, the Janitor Room stays locked to you and the vent is unreachable. Several guides describe it as the single purchase that defines this ending, which is why the 200 Toilet Paper target matters more than any other number in the run.

## Step-by-Step Walkthrough: How to Unlock the Dig to Escape Janitor Ending

Here is the full sequence in order. Treat each row as a checkpoint — if you can hit it, you are on track for the dig to escape janitor ending.

| Step | Action | Checkpoint |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dig underground and collect sellable items | Inventory filling up |
| 2 | Sell everything at the shop | Toilet Paper counter rising |
| 3 | Keep farming until you hold 200 Toilet Paper | 200 TP reached |
| 4 | Buy the Illegal Card | Card in inventory |
| 5 | Head to the Janitor Room and use the card | Door unlocked |
| 6 | Enter the vent | Inside the vent route |
| 7 | Locate and collect 12 batteries | 12/12 collected |
| 8 | Use the batteries to open the final way out | Exit mechanism powered |
| 9 | Walk through and trigger the ending | Ending screen |

### Steps 1–3: Build the Bankroll

The opening phase is identical to a normal run — you dig, you find items, you sell them. The only difference is discipline. Every item you keep "just in case" is Toilet Paper you did not earn. Sell aggressively and track your total against the 200 mark.

### Steps 4–6: Convert Currency Into Access

Once you hit 200, buy the Illegal Card immediately. There is no advantage to holding the currency longer, and a wipe before purchase means the entire grind resets. With the card in hand, move to the Janitor Room and use it to open your path to the vent.

### Steps 7–9: The Battery Sweep

Inside the vent route you are hunting 12 batteries. Sweep methodically rather than sprinting — backtracking costs more time than a slow first pass. When all 12 are in hand, feed them into the mechanism that opens your way out and step through to trigger the ending.

## Fast Toilet Paper Farming Tips

Reaching 200 Toilet Paper is the longest part of the route. These habits, drawn from community reports and general route planning, shave real time off the grind.

| Tip | What to Do | Why It Helps |
|---|---|---|
| Sell on a loop | Empty your inventory every time it fills | Prevents wasted carry space |
| Prioritize high-value digs | Skip low-value clutter when you can identify it | More TP per minute |
| Pick a digging class | Choose speed or capacity over combat perks | The route has almost no combat |
| Stay shallow early | Farm the easier layers before pushing deep | Fewer deaths, steadier income |
| Bank the card early | Buy the Illegal Card the moment you hit 200 | Locks in your progress |

A useful mental model: think in terms of "TP per minute," not "items per run." A fast, safe loop that nets modest value beats a deep, risky run that ends in a wipe. Players who finish this ending usually describe the grind as boring but predictable — and predictable is exactly what you want.

### Prep Checklist Before You Buy

Run through this list before spending your Toilet Paper:

- You are at or above 200 Toilet Paper.
- Your inventory is empty or nearly empty.
- You know the route from your spawn area to the Janitor Room.
- You have not burned your run on a risky deep dig with the currency unspent.
- You have time left in the session to complete the battery sweep.

## Common Mistakes That Waste Runs

Most failed attempts come down to a handful of avoidable errors:

- **Spending Toilet Paper early.** Buying anything other than the Illegal Card delays the run.
- **Entering the vent with a full inventory.** You need room for batteries.
- **Assuming 11 batteries is close enough.** The requirement is 12.
- **Skipping the route check.** Not knowing the way to the Janitor Room costs you the currency you just farmed.
- **Over-diving for value.** Deep layers are risky; the ending does not require them.

Fix those five habits and your clear rate on this route climbs fast.

## FAQ

**How much Toilet Paper do I need for the dig to escape janitor ending?**
You need 200 Toilet Paper, which you spend on the Illegal Card. Farm it by digging up items and selling them at the shop.

**Do I need the Illegal Card to reach the vent?**
Yes. The Illegal Card unlocks your access to the Janitor Room, where the vent is located. Without it, the route is closed.

**How many batteries are required?**
Twelve. Collect all 12 inside the vent area and use them to open the final exit.

**Is the janitor ending good for solo players?**
Community reports say yes. It relies on farming and a short fetch sequence rather than combat or coordinated teamwork, which makes it one of the more solo-friendly routes in the game.

**What is the fastest way to farm 200 Toilet Paper?**
Sell on a loop, pick a digging-focused class, and avoid deep, risky layers. Steady shallow runs add up faster than occasional high-value deep dives that end in a wipe.
