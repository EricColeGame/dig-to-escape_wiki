## What Is the Rooftop Ending in Dig to Escape?

Dig to Escape is built around a simple loop: dig, collect, sell, upgrade, and eventually break out of the building. Different escape routes are gated behind different requirements, and the rooftop route is the one gated behind raw currency rather than reflexes or puzzle knowledge.

Community reports describe the rooftop ending as a newer addition compared to the game's original escapes, which is part of why so many players miss it. Instead of tunneling out or fighting through a scripted sequence, you grind items, sell them, and effectively buy your way into restricted access before finishing on top of the building.

According to [IGN's Dig to Escape endings guide](https://www.ign.com/wikis/dig-to-escape/Endings_Guide_-_How_to_Unlock_All_Endings), the route is refreshingly short once you're funded: dig up items, sell them, save up 300 Toilet Paper, purchase the Illegal Keycard for 200 Toilet Paper, and then make your way to the rooftop access point.

That's it. No boss fight, no timed sequence — just a currency wall that stops most players long before they reach the roof.

## Rooftop Ending Requirements and Toilet Paper Farming

Before you sprint at the roof, confirm you actually have everything the route demands. The table below breaks down the requirements as documented by the source material, plus the practical reasons each one matters.

| Requirement | Amount / Detail | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Toilet Paper banked | 300 total | The full currency target for the route |
| Illegal Keycard | 200 Toilet Paper | The only purchase that unlocks restricted access |

Notice the math: 300 banked, 200 spent. That leaves you a 100 Toilet Paper buffer, which matters more than it sounds. Players who spend down to zero before buying the keycard often find themselves short after a death or a bad sale, then have to restart the grind from scratch.

## Step-by-Step: How to Unlock the Dig to Escape Rooftop Ending

Here is the full route in order. Follow it exactly and you'll avoid the two most common failure points: buying the keycard too early, and wandering the building without knowing where the access point is.

| Step | Action | What to Watch For |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dig up items around the map | Prioritize high-value spawns over easy ones |
| 2 | Sell duplicates and low-value items | Don't hoard — inventory space is progress |
| 3 | Bank 300 Toilet Paper | Resist every non-essential purchase |
| 4 | Buy the Illegal Keycard (200 TP) | Confirm the purchase before leaving the shop |
| 5 | Travel to the rooftop access point | Stay alert — a death can cost you the run |
| 6 | Use the keycard and trigger the ending | Expect a short cinematic to close the route |

A few practical notes that the steps alone don't convey:

- **Do the grind in one session if you can.** Progress resets are the fastest way to lose 200 Toilet Paper worth of effort.
- **Sell before you dig again.** A full inventory means new digs get discarded, which is pure lost income.
- **Treat the keycard as the finish line.** Once it's in your inventory, the rest of the route is travel, not combat.
- **Learn the access point before you need it.** Community reports suggest the final step involves using the keycard at the rooftop access point, though exact placement can shift with game updates.

Once the ending triggers, you'll get the completion credit and can return to the loop for other routes.

## Rooftop Ending vs. Kitchen Ending: Don't Take the Wrong Ramp

This is where most confusion happens. The Kitchen ending also sends players upward — [Insider Gaming's Kitchen ending guide](https://insider-gaming.com/roblox-dig-to-escape-kitchen-ending-guide/) describes climbing five sets of ramps to reach the rooftop, then opening a door to begin that ending's final phase, followed by a short cinematic.

So both routes end with you standing on a roof. That does not make them the same ending. The comparison table below clears it up.

| Feature | Rooftop Ending | Kitchen Ending |
|---|---|---|
| Primary gate | Currency (300 Toilet Paper) | Route navigation and set-piece progression |
| Key item | Illegal Keycard (200 TP) | No keycard purchase required |
| Final action | Use the keycard at rooftop access | Climb five ramp sets, open the door |
| Ending style | Purchased escape | Scripted cinematic sequence |
| Best for | Players who like grinding and planning | Players who like route execution |

If you're standing on a roof and no ending triggered, you're almost certainly on the wrong route. Check whether you're holding an Illegal Keycard — that's the quickest way to tell which ending you're actually pursuing.

One more tip worth internalizing: the rooftop ending rewards patience, not speed. Players who rush the currency target tend to buy the keycard early, die, and lose their buffer. Players who bank a comfortable surplus finish the route in a single clean trip.

## Dig to Escape Rooftop Ending FAQ

**How much Toilet Paper do I need for the dig to escape rooftop ending?**

You need 300 Toilet Paper banked to complete the route comfortably, with 200 of that going toward the Illegal Keycard. The remaining 100 acts as a safety buffer in case you die or make a bad sale before reaching the access point.

**Can I buy the Illegal Keycard before saving the full 300?**

You can, but it's a bad idea. Buying early leaves you with almost no currency buffer, and any setback before you reach the rooftop forces you back into the dig-and-sell loop from a weaker position. Bank first, buy second.

**Is the rooftop ending the same as the Kitchen ending?**

No. Both routes finish high up in the building, which is why players mix them up, but the Kitchen ending is a scripted sequence that involves climbing ramps and opening a door, while the rooftop ending is gated behind currency and an Illegal Keycard purchase. Different gates, different endings.

**Does the rooftop ending ever change?**

Yes — community reports note that the rooftop route is a newer addition to the game, and escape routes, prices, and access points can shift between updates. If the steps above don't match what you're seeing in-game, verify the current requirements before starting a long grind.
