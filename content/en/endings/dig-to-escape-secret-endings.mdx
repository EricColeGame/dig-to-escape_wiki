Here's a workflow that works well in practice:

1. **Clear a room completely** before moving on — including corners that look decorative.

4. **Note what changed** between runs. If a door was locked and now isn't, you're on the right track.

Because these triggers are community-mapped rather than officially documented, details can shift between updates. Cross-check anything you read — including this guide — against the most recent player reports.

## Mistakes That Block Dig to Escape Secret Endings

Most failed attempts come down to a handful of avoidable habits. Fix these and your odds improve dramatically.

| Mistake | Why it hurts | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Spending Toilet Paper immediately | You never reach 300 | Set a hard minimum balance |
| Selling rare items for quick cash | You destroy the trigger item | Keep one of everything |
| Rushing past side rooms | You skip secret areas entirely | Clear rooms fully |
| Stopping after one ending | Other endings stay locked | Replay with intent |

The single biggest offender is the third one. Speed feels productive, but the map rewards curiosity. A room that takes ten extra seconds to search might be the only place a secret-ending item spawns.

The second-biggest offender is selling too aggressively. Toilet Paper is the currency, but rare items are the keys. If you're torn between the two, bank the item and grind a little longer — you can always sell it later, but you can't un-sell it.

Finally, treat each ending as its own objective. Players who unlock Dig to Escape secret endings consistently are the ones who plan a run around a specific goal instead of wandering and hoping something triggers.

## FAQ

**How many endings does Dig to Escape have?**
Published guides describe three endings, while community reports reference additional secret conclusions unlocked through special items and hidden areas. Treat the three as your baseline and the extras as bonus content worth hunting.

**What do I need for the kitchen ending?**
You need 300 Toilet Paper banked, then you need to follow the kitchen route instead of the standard exit. The threshold is the same across routes — only the path and the final trigger differ.

**Are Dig to Escape secret endings permanent?**
They're unlocked through specific in-game actions, not a one-time event. If you complete the requirements again in a fresh run, you should be able to trigger the same conclusion. Keep your trigger items rather than selling them off.

**How do I know which ending I got?**
Watch the final sequence closely. Different endings play out differently, and if the conclusion looks identical to a previous run, you likely repeated the same route rather than finding a new one. If that happens, change your items and approach before retrying.

The bottom line: escaping is easy, but collecting every Dig to Escape secret endings outcome takes patience, curiosity, and a willingness to keep one rare item in your pocket instead of cashing it in.
